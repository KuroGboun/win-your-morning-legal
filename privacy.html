Privacy Policy

win your morning · Effective July 31, 2026

win your morning is a morning-accountability app for small friend groups: you set promises the night before, check in in the morning, and whoever misses is the squad's "Bum of the Day". This policy describes what the app stores and how it's used.

What we collect

  • Account. An anonymous account identifier created when you first open the app. No password is required.
  • Email (optional). If you choose to link an email to your account (Settings → Add an email), we store it so you can recover your account on a new device and so we can send account-related email. You're never required to provide one.
  • Profile. The display name you enter and, if you add one, a profile photo.
  • Activity. Your promises, morning check-ins (won/missed, promises kept, check-in time), streaks and points.
  • Photos. Photos you choose to add (profile photo, "Bum of the Day" photos). Photos are only ever uploaded when you pick or take one yourself.
  • Comments & reactions. Comments and emoji reactions you post on your squad's morning cards.
  • Push notification token. A device-specific identifier used only to deliver push notifications (reminders, comments, reactions) to your device.
  • Reminder settings. Your nightly-reminder time. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

What we don't collect

  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts. Invites are share links you send yourself.

Who can see your data

Your name, photo, promises, check-ins, streaks, points, comments and reactions are visible only to members of the squad you join. Nothing is public. Your email, if you add one, is never shown to other members — it's used only for account recovery. Invite links let anyone who receives them join your squad, so share them only with people you want in your group.

Where it's stored

Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) in the ca-central-1 (Canada) region, and is transmitted over HTTPS.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account. This permanently erases your profile, photos, promises, check-ins and squad memberships. You can also request deletion by email at kuropaypal8@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
